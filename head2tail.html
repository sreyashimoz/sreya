The Paws Behind Our Promise of Care.

At Head2Tail, our directors may handle the big decisions, but let’s be honest—our four-legged boss sets the standards! 🐾 Every decision is guided by one simple rule: happy tails, healthy paws, and pet parents who leave with a little more peace of mind (and maybe a few treats less in their pockets!).

Meet Duke, our handsome Golden Boy, chief tail-wagger, and Head2Tail’s inspiration! 🐾 Overfriendly, full of love for every species, and a true little survivor, Duke’s journey inspired the dream of creating a place where every pet is treated like family.
